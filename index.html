Contract Clause Check

Founders sign contracts they have not fully read. Paste clauses, one per line as section | <clause-text>, and the check flags wording that commonly deserves a second look: broad indemnities, liability without a cap, auto-renewals without a notice window, perpetual IP grants, vague payment terms, and missing termination or governing-law clauses. Heuristic screen, not legal advice.
